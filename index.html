About & How It Works

What problem AgriConnect solves, and how the recommendation logic works in this prototype.

The Problem

Farmers often lack real-time visibility into market prices and buyer demand, leading to distress selling through middlemen at low prices. AgriConnect aims to close this information gap with transparent price discovery and direct market linkages.

How the Recommendation Logic Works (Demo Model)

  1. Signal Collection: Crop, quantity, quality and location inputs are combined with simulated price feeds from nearby markets.
  2. Scoring: Each candidate market is scored on price, distance-based transport cost, and simulated demand strength.
  3. Net Profit Estimation: Gross value minus estimated transport and storage cost gives expected net profit per market.
  4. Best Choice + Confidence: The highest-scoring market/time window is surfaced with a confidence percentage reflecting simulated data consistency.
  5. Buyer Matching: Buyers are ranked by fit to quantity, quality grade and proximity.
In a production system, this logic would be trained on real mandi price feeds (e.g. Agmarknet), weather data, and historical transaction data. In this prototype, all figures are deterministic simulations for demonstration.
